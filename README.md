# Ece Degirmen | Research Portfolio

I'm a senior at UNC Chapel Hill studying Economics (BS) and Data Science (BA), with a minor in Statistics & Analytics. My interests span applied economics, public policy, finance, and business strategy, particularly questions about how people and organizations respond to incentives, uncertainty, and changing conditions. Across these areas, I’m drawn to work that combines economic reasoning, statistical analysis, and programming to understand complex problems and inform practical decisions.

This portfolio brings together my developing applied economics and policy research, computational modeling, and collaborative data analysis. Each project page explains the question, current stage, my contribution, and the next steps. Code, figures, and written outputs will be linked as they are added.

I want readers to be able to follow how a question becomes an analysis: where the data came from, what changed during cleaning, which assumptions matter, and what the results can truly support. The [research workflow](docs/research-workflow.md) sets out how I plan to document and reproduce the work as this repository develops.
